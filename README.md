## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Iris need a constant internet connection?**
Yes. While the software runs on your computer, it connects to remote AI services to process complex tasks.

**Is Iris safe to use?**
Yes. The software follows strict privacy rules. It only accesses the information you provide during your chat sessions.

**Can I delete my chat history?**
Yes. Open the settings menu and select the clear history option. This action removes all saved conversations from your local database.

**Where does Iris save my data?**
Iris stores all your information in a local folder on your Windows machine. You can find this folder in your Documents directory under the Iris subfolder.

**What should I do if the app stops working?**
Restart the application first. If the problem continues, check your internet connection. If you still encounter issues, restart your Windows computer.

## 🧩 Troubleshooting

If the text in the chat window appears blurry or the window does not resize correctly, check your Windows display settings. Ensure that your scaling settings sit at the default level recommended by Windows.

If the AI fails to generate a response, verify that your firewall software allows Iris to communicate with the network. Most modern firewall programs will ask for permission when you first start the application. Click allow to ensure the best experience. 

If you notice high memory usage, close other programs that you are not using. Iris uses a small portion of your system memory to maintain the active conversation state. Closing background applications frees up these resources for the AI model to perform faster.
